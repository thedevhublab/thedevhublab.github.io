About Me

I am a Mathematics and Statistics graduate and currently a Test Engineer at Nike, specializing in data-driven product evaluation and performance analysis. My work focuses on transforming complex testing data into actionable insights that support product innovation, consumer experience, and strategic decision-making.

Throughout my career, I have developed expertise across product testing, consumer consulting, and sports event operations. This diverse background has provided me with a unique perspective on how analytics, consumer behavior, and business strategy intersect to create meaningful outcomes.

At Nike, I manage and analyze footwear performance testing programs, helping translate technical findings into recommendations that improve product quality and consumer value. Prior to Nike, I worked as a Fitness Consultant, where I leveraged customer and membership data to deliver personalized solutions and contributed to a 40% increase in average sales performance.

My passion for sports extends beyond the workplace. As a volunteer at the ISU Short Track World Cup, I collaborated with media representatives and event stakeholders in a fast-paced international environment, strengthening my communication and cross-functional collaboration skills.

I am particularly interested in Consumer Insights, Sports Analytics, Product Marketing, and Sports Business Strategy. My goal is to bridge the gap between data and decision-making by turning analytical findings into strategies that create measurable business impact.
