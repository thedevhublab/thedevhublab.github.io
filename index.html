About Me

I am a Mathematics and Statistics graduate with a strong foundation in analytical thinking and data-driven decision-making. My passion for sports and human performance has led me to gain diverse experiences across product testing, customer consulting, and sports event operations.

Currently, I work as a Test Engineer at Nike, where I manage and analyze footwear performance testing data to support product development decisions. By transforming test results into actionable insights, I help improve both product performance and consumer experience.

Previously, as a Fitness Consultant, I leveraged customer and membership data to deliver personalized recommendations, contributing to a 40% increase in average sales performance. This experience strengthened my understanding of consumer behavior, business strategy, and effective communication.

I also volunteered at the ISU Short Track World Cup, supporting press center operations and collaborating with media representatives and event stakeholders in a fast-paced international environment.

My unique combination of statistical expertise, sports industry knowledge, product evaluation experience, and customer-centric thinking enables me to solve business challenges through data. I am particularly interested in Consumer Insights, Product Marketing, Sports Analytics, and Sports Business Strategy, where I can connect data with meaningful decision-making.
